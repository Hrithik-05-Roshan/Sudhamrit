The Sudhamrit Legacy

A Symphony of
Authentic Spices & Hospitality.

Nestled along the historic Grand Trunk Road in Bastin Bazar, Sudhamrit has stood as a beloved gastronomic destination for families, food lovers, and celebratory gatherings across Asansol.

We preserve age-old regional recipes — from slow-simmering mutton curries cooked over low flames with pure mustard oil, to fresh cottage cheese whipped in cashew-velvet gravy, to fragrant biryanis sealed with handi dough.
